# Building a Training Business from the Documentation

## Stage 1: Free learning material
Publish original beginner lessons, a clear syllabus, selected architecture diagrams, and sample interview questions. Use these to build credibility and receive corrections from learners.

## Stage 2: Structured paid offer
Possible products:
- Live weekend Hadoop/CDP administration cohort
- Original notes, diagrams, quizzes, and lab worksheets
- Guided troubleshooting labs
- Interview preparation sessions

Define audience, prerequisites, outcomes, schedule, support, refund terms, and version scope. Avoid job-placement or salary guarantees.

## Stage 3: Subscription access
GitHub Pages is a public static website; it does not enforce subscriber access. Use a course/membership platform with payment processing and access control, or manage premium content privately while validating demand. Compare current India support, fees, payouts, recurring payments, refunds, tax/invoice features, and learner access management. Never collect card details yourself.

## Suggested model
- Free: syllabus and selected HDFS lessons.
- Low-cost: complete notes, quizzes, and lab worksheets.
- Main offer: live cohort with guided labs and doubt-clearing.
- Premium: limited mentoring or interview preparation.

Start with a small pilot and improve it from learner feedback before spending heavily on video production or paid hosting.
