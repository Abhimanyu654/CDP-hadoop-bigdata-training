# Scenario-Based Questions

- One DataNode is down. How do you assess risk and recovery?
- NameNode HA failover did not occur. What do you check?
- YARN applications remain pending. What do you inspect?
- Spark executors repeatedly fail with memory errors. How do you investigate?
- Hive query fails while Metastore is unavailable. What can still work?
- Impala metadata is stale. What is your safe validation process?
- Ranger KMS is unavailable. Which operations may be impacted?
- Two of three ZooKeeper servers are down. What is the quorum impact?
- Kafka consumer lag grows. What do you measure first?
